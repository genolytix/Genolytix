About the workshop

Artificial Intelligence is rapidly transforming modern healthcare and biological sciences by enabling new approaches in diagnostics, precision medicine, drug discovery, computational biology, genomics and biomedical data analysis. As AI continues to reshape scientific research and clinical practice, interdisciplinary collaboration between computational scientists, biologists, clinicians and technology experts has become increasingly important.

This workshop brings together researchers, academicians, healthcare professionals, industry experts and students to explore recent developments and emerging opportunities at the interface of Artificial Intelligence, healthcare and biological sciences.

Featured application areas

AI in healthcare

Disease prediction, diagnostics, imaging and clinical analytics

Genomics & bioinformatics

Sequence analysis, omics data and precision biology

AI-driven drug discovery

Computational therapeutics and molecular design
